Privacy policy – n8n personal agents

This application is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: Gmail messages, read-only (scope gmail.readonly).
  • Purpose: generating summaries and priority alerts for the owner only.
  • Processing: on the owner's self-hosted server. Message excerpts are sent to a third-party AI model API solely to produce the summary.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Retention: execution logs are kept for 7 days and backups for 14 days, then deleted.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
